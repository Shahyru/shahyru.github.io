Egg Jump Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026 · Applies to the Egg Jump app on Android and iOS

Egg Jump is a single-player game. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone. The only information that leaves your device is the record of in-app purchases, which is needed so purchases work and can be restored.

1Who we are

Egg Jump is made by Shaheer, an independent developer based in the Maldives ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why.

2Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally on your phone so you can continue where you left off: cleared stages and stars, best scores, coins, health, unlocked eggs, your chosen egg and your sound setting. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it.

3In-app purchases

You can buy health packs and the full game (stages 6–10) inside Egg Jump.

  • Payments are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your card details, billing address or account email.
  • Purchase records are managed by RevenueCat, a service that confirms your purchases with Google or Apple. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (which products you bought and when) together with a random app user ID that RevenueCat creates. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • This lets the game deliver what you bought and lets you use Restore purchases to get the full game back after reinstalling or on a new device.
  • Purchase data is encrypted in transit. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone other than the payment stores and RevenueCat.

Read more in Google's Privacy Policy, Apple's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

4Permissions

On Android the game uses internet access only to check and restore purchases, and vibration for tap feedback. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or notification permissions. The game works offline, apart from purchases.

5Children

Egg Jump is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Purchases are made through Google Play or the App Store, where parents can require approval for purchases using Family Link or Ask to Buy.

6Keeping and deleting data

  • Game progress: delete it any time by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage.
  • Purchase records: kept for as long as needed to restore your purchases and meet legal and tax duties. To ask us to delete the purchase record held by RevenueCat, email us using the address below. Deleting it means purchases can no longer be restored to a new device.
  • Your payment history with Google or Apple is controlled by them and can be managed in your Google or Apple account.

7Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it. Because Egg Jump does not collect personal information beyond the anonymous purchase record, most requests can be handled by uninstalling the game or emailing us.

8Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling information differently, for example by adding online leaderboards, we will update this policy and the date at the top before the change goes live.
